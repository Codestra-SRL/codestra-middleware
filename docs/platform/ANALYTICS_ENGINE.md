# Analytics engine

Canonical metrics are impressions, reach, views, likes, reactions, comments, shares, clicks, engagements, video views, watch time, follower delta, conversions, leads and qualified leads. Unsupported values remain null and advertising costs are never inferred.

Snapshots may be taken at 1h, 6h, 24h, 72h and 7d when provider data exists. Optimization outputs KEEP, CHANGE_TIME, CHANGE_COPY, CHANGE_CTA, CHANGE_MEDIA, CHANGE_NETWORK, PAUSE or TEST_VARIANT and always require approval.
