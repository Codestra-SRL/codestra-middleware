# Lead intelligence

Lead classification supports HOT_LEAD, WARM_LEAD, COLD_LEAD, SUPPORT, GENERAL, COMPLAINT, SPAM and NOT_A_LEAD. Scores retain their deterministic factors; opaque AI output cannot be the sole score.

Normalized email, phone or social profile hashes provide tenant-scoped dedupe without logging identifiers. Consent and DNC remain explicit and UNKNOWN never becomes granted. Odoo and dialer handoffs are Middleware commands and remain disabled.
