# Roles

Supported roles are `CALL_CENTER_SUPERVISOR`, `CALL_CENTER_MANAGER`, `QA_REVIEWER`, `QA_MANAGER`, `COMPLIANCE_REVIEWER`, `WORKFORCE_MANAGER`, `CAMPAIGN_VIEWER`, `CAMPAIGN_OPERATOR`, `SUPERVISOR_AUDITOR`, and the non-browser service role `SUPERVISOR_INTEGRATION_SERVICE`. Tenant, workspace and team scope are mandatory regardless of role. Managers may span assigned teams; nobody receives direct downstream access.
