# Revenue attribution

Immutable campaign touches feed versioned calculations. Supported models are first-touch, last-touch, linear, position-based (default 40/20/40), and time-decay (default seven-day half-life). Settings are stored with every calculation, previous versions are marked superseded rather than deleted, and weights always sum to one.

Amounts remain in their authoritative currency. Codestra never sums unlike currencies or fabricates exchange rates. Attribution is evidence, not a replacement for Odoo accounting authority.
