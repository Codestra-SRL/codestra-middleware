# Compliance readiness evidence

Evidence is collected as immutable, hashed control records with owner,
framework, scope, status, reviewer, and timestamp. Retention, legal hold,
classification, export, and deletion decisions remain auditable. “Ready” means
the control matrix, audit trail, retention policy, and restoration evidence are
complete; it is not a certification claim.

The architecture is prepared for SOC 2 and ISO 27001 control mapping, HIPAA and
GDPR/CCPA safeguards, and PCI segmentation where payment scope requires it.
Formal certification, legal interpretation, and customer-specific regulatory
attestation require independent review.
